# Instructions for AI agents and contributors

Before proposing, planning, coding, reviewing, or generating assets for IdleCommand, read these files in order:

1. `docs/foundation/DESIGN_AUTHORITY.md`
2. `docs/foundation/GAME_DESIGN_BIBLE.md`
3. The active milestone document in `roadmap/`

## Mandatory behaviour

- Treat the foundation documents as the project's source of truth.
- Evaluate every feature against the Feature Approval Test in `DESIGN_AUTHORITY.md`.
- Do not reintroduce the retired workshop/productivity-dashboard concept.
- Do not add permanent resource HUDs, XP, quests, direct villager control, attention traps, or progression pressure without an explicit approved amendment.
- Keep active scope limited to Milestone 0.0.1 until its acceptance criteria are met.
- Prefer observable world behaviour over explanatory interface.
- Preserve character autonomy, calm, and safe absence.

## Core identity

> A tiny world that keeps living while you live yours.

> IdleCommand is not trying to steal your attention. It is trying to deserve it.
